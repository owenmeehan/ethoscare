Privacy notice

This website does not keep a database of enquiries, and it should not receive case files.

Ethos Care Ltd is the data controller for information you send about this service. Company number 14814360. ICO registration C1492684. Registered office: 28 Hudson Road, Southsea, Hampshire, PO5 1HD.

What this site collects

Reading these pages does not ask you to create an account. The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not load fonts or videos from another company.

The referral form does not submit to a server here. It opens an email in your own email app, addressed to info@ethoscare.org.uk. What happens to that email then depends on your email provider and on Ethos Care’s email system.

What not to send

Do not use the form, or ordinary email, for a young person’s full name, date of birth, address, photographs or case history. Ask Ethos Care for a secure route. Placement records are handled under the company’s record-keeping practice and the placing authority’s own duties, not through this public page.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of personal information Ethos Care holds about you, and you can ask for corrections. Contact info@ethoscare.org.uk or write to the registered office. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

This notice explains the website. It is not the full privacy notice for residents’ care records. Ask the registered manager for that document.
