Local authorities, charities and other referrers

Ask about a vacancy first. Send the case papers second, and only by a secure route.

Ethos Care takes referrals from local authorities and from other organisations arranging support for a young person. An individual can enquire, but a placement still depends on a proper assessment and a placing authority.

What happens next

  1. 1
    Enquiry. Call or use the form with your name, role, organisation and what you need. Do not include the young person’s identity in that first message.
  2. 2
    Secure papers. If there may be a vacancy, we agree how the referral, risk information and care plan should be sent.
  3. 3
    Compatibility. We assess risk and whether the young person is a safe match for the people already living in Montgomerie House or Hudson House.
  4. 4
    Visit. Where it would help, the placing authority and the young person can visit before a decision.
  5. 5
    Offer or a clear no. We confirm the placement, or we explain why it is not the right house.
Documents you can ask for

Statement of purpose, safeguarding and complaints procedures, staffing arrangements, insurance, and evidence of any registration that applies to the placement. Fees are agreed with the placing authority. There is no public rate card.

Capacity

  • 12 bedrooms across two houses
  • No more than six young people in each house
  • Mixed gender, planned household by household
  • 16 to 24, subject to assessment

What we need to know once a secure channel is open

  • Placing authority, social worker and decision maker
  • Age, legal status and the type of placement sought
  • What a good week would look like, and what would make the placement unsafe
  • Education, health, faith, language and immigration advice already in place
  • Risks to the young person and to other residents, including missing episodes, exploitation, self-harm and offending
  • Who else must be told on the day of a move
