Supported accommodation · Hampshire

A safe house in Southsea, and the support to leave it well.

Ethos Care runs two small supported homes in Southsea, Portsmouth, for young people aged 16 to 24. Care leavers, looked-after young people and unaccompanied asylum-seeking young people live here while they build the skills for a place of their own.

What makes a placement here

Housing is the start. The work is a stable household, a named key worker, and a plan that belongs to the young person.

Somewhere to call home

Lockable rooms, a shared kitchen and lounge, and a private garden. Young people are encouraged to make the room theirs.

Respect for each person

Support is personal. Education, faith, culture, health and the pace of independence are planned with the young person, not for a template.

People who stay involved

Staff are on site day and night. Relationships are built on trust, and the placing authority is kept in the picture.

What we are here to do

These are the aims Ethos Care has set for the service in Portsmouth.

  1. 01A trusted local service

    Support that placing authorities can rely on, explained in plain terms.

  2. 02Safe and respected

    Every young person should feel safe in the house and respected as an individual.

  3. 03Skilled staff

    People recruited for care and skill, trained in trauma-informed practice.

  4. 04Skills for adulthood

    Cooking, money, health, college, work and a future tenancy.

  5. 05A place in the city

    Links into education, health, faith communities and ordinary Portsmouth life.

Southsea

Why this part of Portsmouth

Both homes are in a quiet part of Southsea, with the seafront, colleges, GP surgeries, buses and the train close by. The area has parks, a sports centre, and places of worship used by Christian, Muslim, Sikh, Hindu and other communities. International shops and local festivals make it a practical place to settle, not only a pretty one.

Street addresses are not published on this website. Placing authorities receive them when a referral is being considered.
