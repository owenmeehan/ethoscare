Southsea, Portsmouth

Two three-storey houses, a short walk from the ordinary life of the city.

Montgomerie House and Hudson House sit in the same quiet, mixed part of Southsea. Both are described by Ethos Care in the same way: six double bedrooms, shared bathrooms, and rooms downstairs for cooking, eating and being together.

House one

Montgomerie House

Up to six young people. Mixed gender, matched for safety and compatibility before anyone moves in.

House two

Hudson House

Up to six young people. Same area of Southsea, same offer of room, welcome pack and overnight staff.

The bedroom

Each bedroom locks and is furnished. Residents can personalise a neutral room.

  • Double bed, TV, wardrobe, desk and a personal safe
  • Shared bathroom on each floor, plus a ground-floor toilet
  • Welcome pack: bedding, towels, a calendar and hygiene products

The shared house

Communal rooms are for meals, rest and time with other residents.

  • Open-plan kitchen with a cupboard each for food
  • Dining space, lounge with sofas, a TV and a games console
  • Enclosed private garden
  • Staff office at the front, also used as the sleep-in room

The neighbourhood

Southsea has colleges, GP and dental practices, youth services, leisure centres, parks and a skate park. Buses, trains and council e-scooters make the rest of Portsmouth reachable. Mosques, churches, and Sikh and Hindu temples are part of the local map, alongside other faith communities. Festivals and everyday shops give young people more than one way to belong.

Addresses stay off the website

Publishing where young people sleep would be a safeguarding risk. Placing authorities are given the address, and can arrange a visit, once a referral is being properly considered. The company’s registered office, which is already public, is 28 Hudson Road, Southsea, PO5 1HD.
