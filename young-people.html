Home / Young people

Read this if you might move in

Your room locks. Staff sleep in the house. You do not have to figure Portsmouth out on your own.

This page is for you if you are 16 to 24 and a social worker has talked about Ethos Care. There are two houses in Southsea: Montgomerie House and Hudson House. Up to six young people live in each one.

What you can expect

  • A bedroom with a bed, TV, wardrobe, desk and a safe. You can put your own things up.
  • Bedding, towels and basic hygiene products when you arrive.
  • A kitchen with your own food cupboard, a lounge, and a garden that is just for the house.
  • Shared bathrooms. There is also a toilet on the ground floor.
  • A key worker you see each week, and help on other days when something comes up.
  • Support with college or work, a GP, a dentist, money, cooking and the things you will need for your own place later.
  • If you are seeking asylum, help with language, an interpreter, and reaching the right legal advice. Your faith and culture are part of the plan, not an extra.

Before you move

Staff and your social worker look at who already lives in the house. You should not be moved in if it is not safe for you or for them. You can visit first. Ask your social worker to set that up.

We do not put the house address on the internet. Your social worker will tell you where it is.

If something is wrong

Tell a member of staff or your social worker. If you do not want to do that, you can call Childline free on 0800 1111, any time. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999.

More numbers, including Portsmouth’s safeguarding team, are on the safeguarding page.

You can also phone Ethos Care on 023 9355 2871 or 07581 005131. Someone is available 24 hours a day.
